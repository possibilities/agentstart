# 0009: Keep a conversational front with selective managers

Accepted September 13, 2026 (September 14 UTC). Extends the existing
[manager/worker ownership](0006-own-manager-worker-roles.md) and
[routing evidence](0008-native-history-routing-receipts.md) decisions.

The human accepted a light conversational front with selective strong-manager
ownership, provisionally Sol/low, after the bounded Sol/Terra comparison.
Both model families exercised actual native delegation and steering; forced
manager chains cost more and did not consistently improve source correctness.
The program's evidence is in the wiki report
`router-architecture-with-sol-and-terra-results-and-doctrine-roadmap`.

Clarify the existing manager prompt: keep intent, authority, brief status,
steering and delivery with the lead; delegate substantial interacting or
consequential judgment when useful. A bounded manager owns its result, while
the lead checks decisive evidence and accepts the work. No handoff is required
for every task and a manager's completion is not proof of correctness.
Assess accepted outcomes and total workflow resources, including review and
retries. Weakest-model qualification is not an architecture prerequisite.

Keep model selection separate from the general role. Sol/low is provisional,
not a claim of universal quality or quota savings. No new router role, worker
inventory change, native child-role injection, live model switch or restart
follows from the prompt delivery. Resource sync prepares future loads;
already-loaded generations and captured workspace role databases retain their
contents. Phase 2/HUD implementation and training/export remain separate.

Role rendering, document integrity and installed-output checks establish
delivery of the instruction. They do not establish behavior in every task or
live conversational timing.
